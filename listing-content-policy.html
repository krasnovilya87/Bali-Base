BaliBase Listing & Content Policy

Effective Date: 10.08.2026
Last Updated: 10.08.2026

This Listing & Content Policy (“Policy”) governs listings, reviews, photographs, profiles, advertisements and other content submitted to BaliBase, available at balibase.id.

This Policy forms part of the BaliBase Terms of Use.

By publishing or submitting content to BaliBase, you agree to comply with this Policy.

Questions or reports may be sent to support@balibase.id.

1. Purpose

BaliBase is intended to help people discover legitimate products, properties, services, employment opportunities, businesses, events and other opportunities.

Our goal is to maintain a useful and reasonably safe Platform while allowing a broad range of lawful activity.

BaliBase may moderate, restrict or remove content that violates applicable law, creates unreasonable fraud or harm risk, materially misleads users, violates third-party rights, undermines Platform integrity or violates this Policy.

2. Responsibility of Listing Providers

The person or organization submitting a listing is responsible for the listing.

By publishing content, you represent that you have authority to offer the advertised product, property, service or opportunity; your listing is materially accurate; you have necessary rights to use photographs and text; the activity is lawful; you hold required licenses or permits; the listing does not intentionally mislead users; and you will keep material information reasonably up to date.

BaliBase's acceptance of a listing does not transfer responsibility from the Listing Provider to BaliBase.

3. General Accuracy Requirement

Listings must not contain materially false or misleading information, including misleading information concerning identity, ownership, price, availability, location, condition, size, facilities, qualifications, licensing, experience, discounts, investment returns, guarantees, included services or photographs.

Minor mistakes should be corrected promptly after discovery. Deliberate deception may result in immediate removal or account suspension.

4. Accurate Prices

Prices should be stated clearly. Listings must not advertise a price that the provider does not genuinely intend to make available merely to attract inquiries.

Where additional mandatory charges apply, providers should disclose material charges reasonably clearly, including taxes, cleaning fees, mandatory service fees, deposits, delivery charges or mandatory rental fees. Optional upgrades may be priced separately.

5. Availability

Providers should make reasonable efforts to keep availability information current.

A listing should not deliberately advertise unavailable inventory solely to redirect users toward another product or a higher price. BaliBase understands that availability may change quickly and does not require perfect real-time accuracy unless explicitly stated for a particular feature.

6. Photographs

Photographs must reasonably represent the product, property, service or business being advertised.

Users must not knowingly use photographs in a way that materially misrepresents condition, size, view, location, facilities or product appearance. Stock photographs may be used only where they do not materially mislead users.

Where photographs belong to another person, the Listing Provider must have permission or another lawful basis to use them.

7. Duplicate Listings

BaliBase may restrict duplicate or substantially identical listings, including repeated listings of the same property by the same provider, repeated products uploaded to manipulate search, multiple accounts advertising the same inventory or repeated listings created to bypass moderation.

Legitimate separate listings may be allowed where they represent distinct rooms, units, products, vehicle models, services, locations or inventory. BaliBase may merge, remove or deprioritize duplicates.

8. Multiple Agents

A property or product may sometimes lawfully be represented by multiple agents. BaliBase does not guarantee exclusivity.

However, a person claiming to represent property or inventory must genuinely have authority to do so. False claims of exclusivity may result in moderation action.

9. Property Listings

Property listings may include hotels, villas, houses, apartments, rooms, guesthouses, commercial property, land, long-term rentals, short-term rentals and property sales.

A provider must have a lawful basis to advertise the property. A listing must not falsely claim legal ownership, lease authority, title, permits, land rights, guaranteed development rights or guaranteed foreign ownership rights.

Materially disputed ownership may be grounds for temporary removal while BaliBase reviews relevant information.

10. Property Investment Claims

Investment-related property listings must not guarantee future financial performance unless the claim can lawfully and objectively be guaranteed.

Claims requiring particular caution include “guaranteed ROI”, “risk-free”, “guaranteed resale price”, “guaranteed appreciation”, “100% legal ownership for foreigners” and “guaranteed passive income”.

Forecasts should be clearly identified as estimates or projections where appropriate. Past performance does not automatically establish future performance.

11. Vehicle Listings

Users may list lawful vehicles for sale, rental, lease and related services.

Providers are responsible for complying with requirements concerning ownership, vehicle registration, rental authorization, insurance, licensing, roadworthiness and taxes. Listings must not knowingly conceal serious safety defects.

12. Services

BaliBase may permit a broad range of lawful services, including cleaning, construction, repairs, fitness, beauty, transportation, tourism, education, consulting, legal services, immigration services, financial services, health services and professional services.

A provider is responsible for determining whether the service requires a professional license or other authorization.

13. Medical and Health Services

Medical or health-related services may be listed only where lawful.

Providers must not falsely claim medical qualifications, licenses, certifications, guaranteed cures or guaranteed medical outcomes. Dangerous or unlawful medical services may be removed.

BaliBase does not verify every practitioner's professional status.

14. Legal and Immigration Services

Legal, visa, immigration and related services may be advertised where lawful.

Providers must not falsely represent themselves as licensed lawyers or government officials, guarantee visas or immigration decisions they cannot control, falsely imply affiliation with an embassy or government agency, or sell forged immigration documents.

15. Financial Services

Lawful financial services may be permitted subject to applicable regulation.

Listings must not offer unauthorized banking services, unlawful lending, money laundering, stolen payment credentials, fraudulent credit services or deceptive financial schemes. BaliBase may require additional verification for certain financial categories.

16. Investments

Investment and business-opportunity listings may be subject to increased moderation.

Listing Providers must not use BaliBase to promote Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, fake investment funds, fraudulent securities, false or misleading guaranteed returns, fabricated revenue, fabricated ownership, fabricated financial records or undisclosed impersonation of another company.

Users publishing investment opportunities remain responsible for compliance with all applicable investment, corporate, securities and financial laws.

17. BaliBase Does Not Endorse Investments

The publication, moderation or verification of an investment-related listing does not mean that BaliBase recommends the investment, confirms profitability, confirms legal ownership, confirms valuation, confirms financial statements, confirms regulatory compliance or guarantees returns.

BaliBase may apply enhanced verification without becoming responsible for the underlying investment.

18. Jobs and Employment

Job listings must relate to genuine work opportunities.

Prohibited recruitment practices may include human trafficking, forced labor, recruitment scams, fake employers, requests for unlawful payments, deceptive salary claims and exploitation.

Employers remain responsible for visas, work permits, contracts and compliance with employment law.

19. Events

Users may advertise lawful events. Event organizers are responsible for permits, venue authority, safety, ticketing, age restrictions, licenses, intellectual property and regulatory compliance.

20. Alcohol

Listings involving alcohol may be permitted where lawful. Providers are responsible for required licenses, age restrictions, advertising restrictions, delivery rules, local restrictions and sales requirements.

BaliBase may restrict alcohol-related content based on law, safety requirements or Platform policy. Listing alcohol on BaliBase does not constitute confirmation that a seller is licensed.

21. Tobacco, Nicotine and Other Restricted Products

BaliBase may restrict or prohibit listings involving cigarettes, tobacco, vaping products, nicotine products and other age-restricted products.

Such categories may be subject to stricter Platform rules than the minimum required by law. BaliBase may modify these restrictions based on regulatory or safety considerations.

22. Drugs

BaliBase prohibits listings involving unlawful narcotics, controlled substances or recreational drugs.

This includes content seeking to sell, purchase, distribute, deliver, manufacture or facilitate unlawful drug transactions. Listings for lawful medical products may be subject to separate requirements.

23. Medicines

Prescription medicines or regulated medicines may only be advertised where the activity is lawful and appropriately authorized.

BaliBase may prohibit direct user-to-user sales of regulated medicines regardless of whether a particular transaction could theoretically be lawful.

24. Weapons and Dangerous Items

Users must not list weapons or dangerous items where their sale, possession or advertising violates applicable law or BaliBase policy.

BaliBase may prohibit firearms, ammunition, explosives, illegal weapons, prohibited knives, weapon components and bomb-making materials, and may apply stricter restrictions than required by minimum law.

25. Counterfeit Goods

Counterfeit products are prohibited. Users may not advertise goods falsely bearing another person's trademark, logo, brand or authenticity marks.

Listings for replicas or imitations may also be removed where they infringe intellectual-property rights or mislead users.

26. Stolen Property

Users must not use BaliBase to sell, rent or distribute stolen property.

If BaliBase reasonably suspects that property is stolen, it may remove the listing, suspend the account, preserve relevant information and cooperate with lawful authorities where appropriate.

27. Identity Documents

Users must not sell or facilitate unlawful transfer of passports, identity cards, driving licenses, government credentials, immigration documents, forged certificates or forged licenses.

Legitimate document-processing services may be listed where lawful.

28. Fraud

Fraud is prohibited. Examples include fake rental properties, fake businesses, fake vehicle listings, advance-fee scams, deposit scams, payment impersonation, fake escrow, fabricated ownership, fabricated invoices, fake employment, investment scams, account takeover and phishing.

BaliBase may immediately suspend accounts where serious fraud is reasonably suspected.

29. Financial Crime

Users must not use BaliBase for money laundering, terrorist financing, fraudulent payment processing, stolen financial accounts, payment-card theft, unlawful cash conversion or other financial crime.

30. Gambling

Illegal gambling services are prohibited. BaliBase may also restrict gambling-related advertising even where some form of gambling may be lawful in another jurisdiction.

31. Sexual Services

BaliBase may prohibit listings offering sexual services, prostitution, sexual exploitation or other sexual commercial activity that violates applicable law or Platform rules.

Sex trafficking, exploitation and non-consensual sexual material are strictly prohibited.

32. Child Safety

Any content involving sexual exploitation or abuse of children is strictly prohibited.

BaliBase may immediately remove content, disable accounts, preserve evidence and report matters to competent authorities where legally appropriate.

33. Human Trafficking

Listings involving human trafficking, forced labor, slavery, exploitation or unlawful recruitment are prohibited.

34. Wildlife and Protected Species

Users must not advertise products, animals or services involving illegal wildlife trade or protected species in violation of applicable law.

BaliBase may restrict wildlife-related categories where lawful status cannot reasonably be determined.

35. Animals

Listings involving animals may be permitted only where lawful and consistent with BaliBase policy.

BaliBase may restrict protected species, illegal wildlife, abusive breeding, animal fighting and other harmful or unlawful activity.

36. Cybercrime

Users may not offer or request unlawful cyber services, including hacking, malware, credential theft, phishing, unauthorized account access, denial-of-service attacks or stolen databases.

Legitimate cybersecurity services may be permitted where their lawful purpose is clear.

37. Personal Data

Listings must not unlawfully publish another person's personal information.

Users should not publish passports, bank account credentials, private addresses unrelated to a listing, passwords, identity documents, private correspondence or sensitive personal information without lawful authority.

38. Harassment and Threats

Content may be removed where it constitutes credible threats, stalking, targeted harassment, extortion, blackmail or serious intimidation.

39. Hate and Violence

BaliBase may remove content that unlawfully promotes or incites violence or serious discrimination against protected groups.

This rule is not intended to prohibit ordinary lawful discussion or criticism.

40. Illegal Content Generally

A listing may be removed where BaliBase reasonably believes it violates applicable law even if the exact category is not specifically identified in this Policy.

BaliBase is not required to create an exhaustive catalogue of every prohibited item under every law. Users remain responsible for determining whether their activity is lawful.

41. Intellectual Property

Users must respect intellectual-property rights. Content may be removed where it infringes copyright, trademarks, design rights or other legally protected intellectual-property rights.

Copyright complaints may be sent to support@balibase.id.

42. False Reviews

Users must not create fake reviews, purchase fake reviews, review themselves through alternate accounts, coordinate review manipulation, threaten negative reviews to obtain money or offer undisclosed payment for positive reviews.

43. Reviews Are Not Removed Merely Because They Are Negative

A business or provider does not have a right to remove a review merely because the review is negative, the rating is low or the provider disagrees with the opinion.

However, BaliBase may remove reviews that are fake, spam, abusive, irrelevant, unlawful, legally actionable as defamatory, privacy-invasive, threatening, manipulated or created in violation of Platform rules.

44. External Google Reviews

Google ratings and reviews displayed through authorized integration remain third-party content. BaliBase may not be able to edit or remove the underlying Google review itself.

Requests concerning underlying Google content may need to be directed to Google.

45. Advertising and Promotion

Paid promotion does not exempt a listing from this Policy. Promoted listings remain subject to the same or stricter moderation standards as ordinary listings.

BaliBase may suspend promotion immediately if the underlying listing violates Platform rules.

46. BaliBase Verified

Verification status does not give a user permission to violate this Policy. Verified accounts and listings remain subject to continuing moderation.

BaliBase may remove verification where information changes, complaints arise, documents appear unreliable, the provider violates Platform rules or new risks are discovered.

47. AI Moderation

BaliBase may use AI to identify possible prohibited items, fraudulent text, duplicate listings, suspicious images, misleading claims, spam and policy violations.

AI decisions may be reviewed manually where appropriate. BaliBase is not required to disclose technical fraud-detection methods where doing so could help users circumvent them.

48. Manual Moderation

BaliBase may manually review listings, profiles, photographs, reports, verification documents, reviews and suspicious behavior.

Moderators may request additional information. Failure to provide requested information may result in removal where BaliBase cannot reasonably establish that a listing should remain available.

49. Reports

Users are encouraged to use Report Listing or other reporting mechanisms if they suspect fraud, illegal activity, inaccurate content, duplicate listings, stolen content, scams or safety concerns.

Reports should be made in good faith. Abusive or deliberately false reporting may itself violate BaliBase rules.

50. Moderation Actions

Depending on the situation, BaliBase may request edits or evidence, warn the user, limit visibility, remove or temporarily hide content, remove verification, restrict contact functionality, suspend an account, permanently ban an account or block identifiers associated with serious abuse.

The response may depend on severity, history and available information.

51. Immediate Removal

BaliBase may act without prior warning where there is a reasonable concern involving serious fraud, illegal content, safety risks, cybercrime, exploitation, threats, stolen property, repeated abuse, serious intellectual-property violations or attempts to evade prior suspension.

52. Removal Without Proven Violation

BaliBase is not a court and is not required to establish a legal violation beyond doubt before removing content.

BaliBase may remove or restrict content where continued publication reasonably presents fraud risk, legal risk, security risk, user-safety risk or Platform-integrity risk.

53. No Right to Permanent Publication

Submitting a listing does not create an unconditional right to have it published indefinitely.

BaliBase controls the organization and operation of its Platform and may change categories, search ranking, listing formats, eligibility rules, moderation rules and visibility requirements.

54. Search Ranking

BaliBase may determine search order using factors including relevance, freshness, completeness, location, availability, quality, verification, user activity, reliability signals and paid promotion.

BaliBase does not guarantee a particular search position.

55. Circumvention

Users must not attempt to bypass BaliBase enforcement through new accounts, alternative phone numbers, alternative emails, altered listings, repeated uploads or technical manipulation.

BaliBase may connect accounts using technical and behavioral indicators for enforcement purposes.

56. Account Blacklisting

Serious or repeated violations may result in internal blacklisting or fraud-prevention restrictions associated with email addresses, phone numbers, account identifiers, IP addresses, devices or other relevant identifiers.

Blacklist information is not intended to be published to other users.

57. Cooperation With Law Enforcement

Where legally permitted or required, BaliBase may cooperate with competent authorities concerning suspected fraud, trafficking, threats, cybercrime, illegal goods, serious financial crime or other unlawful conduct.

58. Enforcement Discretion

BaliBase may consider relevant circumstances when applying this Policy. Similar cases may occasionally result in different outcomes due to differences in available evidence, account history, legal requirements, severity, user safety or context.

BaliBase is not required to apply the maximum possible penalty in every case.

59. Appeals and Questions

Users may contact BaliBase regarding moderation decisions at support@balibase.id.

BaliBase may review a decision but does not guarantee that every moderation decision will be reversed or independently appealed. BaliBase is not required to disclose internal fraud-detection techniques, confidential reports or personal data belonging to other users.

60. Changes to This Policy

BaliBase may update this Policy as Platform categories change, new risks arise, laws change, paid services are introduced or moderation technology develops.

The current version will be published on balibase.id.

61. Relationship With the Terms of Use

This Policy supplements the BaliBase Terms of Use.

If content violates this Policy, BaliBase may take action under both this Policy and the Terms of Use. Nothing in this Policy requires BaliBase to permit content merely because that content is not expressly listed as prohibited.

62. Core Principle

BaliBase is intended for lawful, genuine and reasonably transparent listings.

Listing Providers remain responsible for the legality and accuracy of their activities.

BaliBase may moderate content to protect users and Platform integrity, but publication, moderation or verification does not constitute a guarantee, endorsement or legal certification by BaliBase.
