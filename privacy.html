BaliBase Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 10.08.2026
Last Updated: 10.08.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BaliBase, available at balibase.id (“BaliBase”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, stores, shares and otherwise processes personal data.

BaliBase is currently operated by an individual Platform Operator.

For privacy questions or requests, contact:

support@balibase.id

By using BaliBase, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the BaliBase Terms of Use and Listing & Content Policy.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data processed in connection with balibase.id, BaliBase user accounts, listings, reviews, reporting systems, verification services, location-based services, contact requests, moderation, fraud prevention, customer support, paid services, advertising, analytics and other BaliBase functions.

It does not govern personal data processed independently by third-party services such as WhatsApp, Google or external payment providers.

2. Data Controller

For purposes of applicable data protection law, the operator of BaliBase acts as the controller or equivalent responsible party for personal data processed by BaliBase.

The Platform is currently operated by an individual Platform Operator.

BaliBase
Website: balibase.id
Email: support@balibase.id

BaliBase does not publish the private residential address of the Platform Operator unless disclosure becomes legally required.

3. Personal Data We Collect

The information BaliBase collects depends on how you use the Platform.

3.1 Account Information

When you create or manage an account, we may collect:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • profile image;
  • language preference;
  • account identifier;
  • authentication information;
  • account status;
  • account creation date;
  • login activity.

Passwords are intended to be stored using appropriate secure authentication mechanisms rather than in readable plain-text form.

4. Google Sign-In

Users may be able to create or access an account using Google authentication.

Where Google Sign-In is used, BaliBase may receive limited account information made available through the authentication process, such as name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier.

BaliBase does not request access to unrelated Google account content solely because a user signs in with Google.

Your use of Google services remains subject to Google's own terms and privacy practices.

5. Telephone Numbers

A telephone number may be required to create certain listings, publish listings, update listings, verify an account, reduce duplicate accounts, prevent fraud, enable external contact through WhatsApp and protect users and Platform integrity.

A telephone number is not normally displayed directly on a BaliBase listing unless the user independently publishes it in public content.

However, where a user selects Contact, WhatsApp, or similar functionality, the relevant phone number may become visible through the external WhatsApp service.

Users should understand this before using external contact functionality.

6. Listing Information

When a user creates or manages a listing, BaliBase may collect and process listing title, description, category, location, photographs, videos, prices, availability, property information, vehicle information, service information, business information, employment information, contact information, verification information, listing history, edits and updates.

Information intentionally included in a public listing may be visible to any visitor to BaliBase and may be indexed by search engines.

Users should not include sensitive personal information in public listing descriptions unless necessary and lawful.

7. Location Information

With browser or device permission, BaliBase may access location information.

This may include location information derived from browser geolocation, GPS or device location services, network information, approximate IP-based location and user-selected map locations.

Precise location access will be requested through the relevant browser or device permission mechanism where required.

Users may deny or withdraw location permission through browser or device settings.

Certain location-based functionality may then become unavailable or less accurate.

8. Technical and Device Information

When you access BaliBase, we may automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, device identifiers where available, language, time zone, referring URL, pages viewed, date and time of access, session information, errors, performance information and security events.

This information may be used for security, fraud prevention, analytics and Platform operation.

9. Usage Information

BaliBase may collect information about how users interact with the Platform, including searches, listing views, categories viewed, saved or favorite listings, clicks, contact requests, navigation patterns, advertisement interactions, reporting activity, moderation interactions and account activity.

This information may be associated with an account, device or browser where permitted.

10. Favorites

If users save listings or other content to favorites, BaliBase may store the listing saved, the user account, the time of the action and changes to the user's favorites.

This information may be used to provide personalized Platform functions.

11. Contact Requests

BaliBase does not currently provide a general internal user-to-user chat service.

When a user clicks a contact function that redirects to WhatsApp, BaliBase may retain information about the contact request, such as requesting user, relevant listing, recipient, date and time, and technical information relating to the request.

BaliBase does not ordinarily receive or store the subsequent contents of WhatsApp conversations.

Users communicating through WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's independent privacy terms and practices.

12. Reports and Complaints

When you submit a report or complaint, BaliBase may collect your account information, the listing or user reported, reason for the report, written explanation, supporting materials, date and time, moderation decision and correspondence regarding the complaint.

This information may be used for investigation, safety, fraud prevention, dispute handling and enforcement.

13. Reviews

Where BaliBase offers reviews, we may process reviewer account, reviewed listing or provider, rating, review text, date, moderation status and associated transaction or interaction information where available.

Reviews may be publicly displayed.

Users should not include unnecessary personal data about another person in reviews.

14. Google Reviews and Third-Party Content

BaliBase may display ratings, reviews, business information or other data obtained through authorized third-party APIs, including Google services.

Such information may be subject to the terms, technical restrictions and privacy practices of the relevant third-party provider.

BaliBase may store or cache certain third-party data where permitted by the applicable provider and applicable law.

15. Verification Information

BaliBase may offer verification of accounts, listings or selected listing information.

Depending on the verification method, BaliBase may process information such as telephone number, email, property information, business information, photographs, supporting documents, proof of relationship to a property or business, verification results, verification date and internal verification notes.

BaliBase will seek to collect only information reasonably necessary for the relevant verification.

Verification documents are not intended to become publicly available unless specifically disclosed.

Where possible, information that is no longer necessary may be deleted, restricted or retained only in reduced form.

16. Fraud Prevention and Blacklists

BaliBase may process information for security, abuse prevention and fraud prevention.

This may include telephone numbers, email addresses, IP addresses, account identifiers, Google account identifiers, device or network signals, duplicate-account indicators, reports, moderation history, verification history, suspicious activity and prior bans or restrictions.

BaliBase may maintain internal restricted-account or blacklist records where reasonably necessary to prevent users from repeatedly creating accounts after serious fraud, abuse or suspension.

Such records are not intended to be publicly searchable.

BaliBase may retain limited fraud-prevention information even after an account has been deleted where permitted by applicable law.

17. Artificial Intelligence

BaliBase may use artificial intelligence and automated systems to assist with text moderation, translation, categorization, duplicate detection, spam detection, fraud detection, listing quality review, search, recommendation systems and safety analysis.

Content submitted to BaliBase may therefore be processed automatically.

Where third-party AI providers are used, relevant data may be transmitted to such providers only as reasonably necessary for the relevant service and subject to appropriate contractual or technical safeguards where required.

BaliBase will seek to avoid transmitting unnecessary personal data to AI systems.

Automated systems may make errors, and BaliBase may use human review where appropriate.

18. Cookies and Similar Technologies

BaliBase may use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the Platform.

Essential Cookies

Used for account sessions, authentication, security, preferences and basic Platform functionality.

Analytics Technologies

May be used to understand number of visitors, page usage, performance, traffic sources and user behavior.

Advertising Technologies

If advertising is introduced, BaliBase may use technologies for advertising measurement, campaign performance, audience measurement and personalized advertising where legally permitted.

BaliBase may in the future use service providers such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, advertising platforms or similar services.

Where legally required, non-essential technologies may be subject to user consent.

A dedicated cookie consent interface or Cookie Policy may be introduced as Platform functionality develops.

19. Why We Process Personal Data

BaliBase may process personal data for purposes including creating accounts, authenticating users, operating listings, providing Platform features, enabling WhatsApp contact, providing location functionality, providing favorites, displaying reviews, conducting verification, responding to user requests, customer support, fraud prevention, security, moderation, enforcing the Terms, investigating complaints, preventing duplicate accounts, preventing misuse, improving search, improving recommendations, analytics, troubleshooting, development, legal compliance, handling disputes, establishing or defending legal claims, providing paid services, advertising BaliBase and communicating important service information.

20. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law requires BaliBase to identify a legal basis, processing may be based on one or more of the following:

Consent

For example, precise browser location, certain cookies, optional marketing and other processing where consent is appropriate.

Performance of a Contract

Processing necessary to create and operate an account, publish listings, provide requested features and provide purchased BaliBase services.

Legal Obligations

Processing necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal orders or regulatory obligations.

Legitimate Interests or Other Lawful Interests

Where permitted, BaliBase may process information for legitimate purposes such as protecting users, preventing fraud, maintaining Platform security, detecting duplicate accounts, improving the Platform, preventing abuse and establishing or defending legal claims.

BaliBase seeks to balance these interests against the rights and interests of affected individuals.

21. Public Information

Certain information submitted to BaliBase is intended to be public.

This may include listings, listing photos, descriptions, business names, public profile information, prices, ratings, reviews and locations selected for listings.

Public content may be viewed without an account, shared by users, displayed in search results, indexed by search engines, cached by external services and used to promote BaliBase in accordance with the Terms.

Users should assume that information intentionally placed in a public listing may become widely accessible.

22. How We Share Personal Data

BaliBase does not sell ownership of users' personal data.

We may disclose data where reasonably necessary to service providers assisting with hosting, cloud infrastructure, authentication, databases, security, analytics, email, maps, AI, support, payment processing and technical services; third-party services requested by the user, such as WhatsApp; other users where information is intentionally public or needed for a requested function; authorities where required or permitted by law; safety and fraud prevention purposes; and business transfers if BaliBase is incorporated, reorganized, financed, sold, merged, acquired or transferred.

23. Hosting and International Data Transfers

BaliBase's core data infrastructure may be hosted in Singapore.

Users located in Indonesia or other countries should therefore understand that their information may be transmitted to, stored in or processed in Singapore.

BaliBase may also use service providers located in other jurisdictions.

Where applicable law imposes requirements for international transfers of personal data, BaliBase will seek to use an appropriate transfer mechanism, safeguard, contractual arrangement, consent mechanism or other legally permitted basis.

International transfers do not change BaliBase's commitment to processing personal information according to this Privacy Policy.

24. Data Security

BaliBase uses reasonable organizational and technical measures intended to protect personal data.

Measures may include access controls, authentication, restricted administrative access, encryption where appropriate, secure hosting, logging, backups, monitoring, fraud detection and software security practices.

No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for protecting their account credentials and should notify BaliBase if they suspect unauthorized access.

25. Retention of Personal Data

BaliBase retains personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, subject to applicable law.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

Active Accounts

Account data may generally be retained while the account remains active.

Listings

Listing information may be retained while active and for a reasonable period after removal for administration, abuse prevention, dispute handling or record keeping.

Reports and Moderation Records

May be retained after account deletion where needed to investigate misconduct or prevent repeated abuse.

Anti-Fraud Data

Certain identifiers may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary to prevent serious fraud, circumvention or security risks.

Legal Records

Information may be retained where required for legal claims, taxation, accounting, regulatory requests, court proceedings, law enforcement and contractual disputes.

Backups

Deleted data may temporarily remain in secure backups until those backups are overwritten according to normal retention cycles.

Where continued identification is unnecessary, BaliBase may anonymize or aggregate information.

26. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their BaliBase account.

Account deletion may result in removal or deactivation of profile information, active listings, favorites and certain account-associated data.

Deletion does not necessarily result in immediate deletion of every record.

BaliBase may retain information where reasonably necessary and permitted by applicable law for fraud prevention, blacklist enforcement, security, dispute resolution, legal claims, compliance obligations, financial records and enforcement history.

BaliBase may use automated processing to identify spam, duplicate listings, suspicious accounts, fraud indicators, policy violations, relevant categories, search relevance and recommendations.

Where applicable law gives users specific rights concerning decisions based solely on automated processing that produce significant legal or similarly significant effects, BaliBase will seek to respect those rights.

Most BaliBase moderation tools are intended to assist Platform operation and do not necessarily constitute legally significant automated decision-making.

30. Children

BaliBase is not intended for account use by persons under 18 years of age.

Persons under 18 should not create accounts, publish listings or submit personal information through account functionality.

If BaliBase becomes aware that personal information was collected from a child contrary to applicable requirements, BaliBase may remove the account and associated information.

31. Sensitive Personal Information

Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless necessary for a specific legitimate purpose.

Sensitive information may include, depending on applicable law, health information, biometric information, genetic information, criminal records, financial information, government identity documents and other specially protected categories.

Where such information is required for verification or another legitimate purpose, BaliBase will seek to limit processing to what is reasonably necessary.

Users should not publish identity documents, bank information or other sensitive information in public listing descriptions.

Marketing communications may be sent where permitted.
